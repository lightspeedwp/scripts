# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

You are a contributor. Follow our community standards and treat all participants respectfully. Avoid harassment, trolling, or exclusionary practices.

## Our Pledge

All members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior:

- Welcoming and inclusive language
- Respectful of viewpoints
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism

Unacceptable behavior:

- Insults, trolling, harassment

## Enforcement

Report violations to <support@lightspeedwp.com>.
